About This Game

Feed the worms

Buy food and strange objects, then watch your worms devour them pixel by pixel.

Turn poop into profit

Your worms produce different valuable resources. Collect them, make money!

Push production higher

Buy even more worms! Upgrade their speed, appetite, digestion, and resource production.

Skill Tree!

Spend Colony Points on active abilities and powerful upgrades, unlock new workers to automate your business, and make your worms even more profitable.

Start again. Grow stronger.

Consume your colony, earn permanent upgrades, and rebuild a more efficient worm empire.

AND MUCH MORE!

Discover new worms, abilities, objects, upgrades, and increasingly irresponsible business decisions.

Wishlist Worm Capitalist so you don’t miss the full release!
