Which game is this?

Worm Capitalist is Tikotey’s incremental game. The itch.io listing provides an HTML5/Windows early demo; the Steam listing offers a demo while the full game remains unreleased. This guide concerns that demo.

The developer describes feeding worms, collecting resources, improving production, spending Colony Points, unlocking workers and resetting for permanent upgrades. Those descriptions are an overview, not a verified table of every demo feature or value.
