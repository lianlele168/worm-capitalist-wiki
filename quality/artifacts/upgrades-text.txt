Available evidence

The developer describes speed, appetite, digestion and resource-production upgrades. No numerical upgrade model is reproduced here.

A comparison you can make

Write down the exact upgrade label and cost displayed in your build. Measure a baseline, buy one upgrade, then repeat with similar play behavior. Include the purchase cost if measuring the immediate net balance effect; exclude it from both intervals only when comparing subsequent operating income.

No universal priority

The previous buy-a-worm-first rule was unsupported and has been withdrawn. A useful comparison needs your current version, costs and repeated observations.
